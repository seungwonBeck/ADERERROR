04

FAQ

Answers to common questions about shipping, exchanges, returns and our products.

How can I cancel or change my order?

If your order has not been prepared for shipment, contact customer service and we will help you cancel or change the option. Orders that have already shipped can be handled through our exchange and return process after delivery.

How long do I have to return or exchange an item?

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving the item. Returns may be restricted if the item has been worn, washed or damaged, or if the tags have been removed.

Who pays for return shipping?

For defective or incorrectly shipped items, we cover the shipping cost. For exchanges or returns due to a change of mind, the customer covers the shipping cost.

How do I check the size?

Please refer to the size chart on each product page. Measurements may vary by 1–3 cm depending on the measuring method.

Will sold-out items be restocked?

Restock information will be announced in Notice once confirmed.

Privacy Policy

How personal information is collected, used and protected.

For orders and delivery we collect your name, contact details, shipping address and payment-related information. Access logs, cookies and device information may be collected automatically while you use the service.

2. Purpose of use

We use it for order processing and delivery, payment and refunds, responding to inquiries, improving the service and preventing misuse.

3. Retention period

Personal information is destroyed without delay once its purpose has been fulfilled. Under applicable law, records of contracts or withdrawals and of payments and supply of goods are kept for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints or disputes for 3 years.

We do not provide personal information to third parties except with consent or as required by law. We may outsource tasks such as delivery and payment processing to couriers and payment providers within the scope necessary, and will disclose this in this policy.

Once the retention period has passed or the purpose is fulfilled, electronic files are deleted in a way that prevents recovery, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.
